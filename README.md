## Project Abstract

The Smart Water Tank Overflow Protection and Pump Control System is designed to monitor the water level in a tank and automatically control the water pump based on the detected water level. The system aims to prevent water overflow, reduce water wastage, and provide efficient control of the water pumping process.

The project is designed and simulated using a circuit simulation environment to demonstrate the operation of the water-level monitoring and automatic pump control system under different water-level conditions.

## Project Objectives

- To design a smart water tank monitoring system.
- To detect different water levels in the tank.
- To automatically control the water pump based on the water level.
- To prevent water tank overflow.
- To reduce unnecessary water wastage.
- To simulate and verify the operation of the proposed system.

## System Features

- Water-level monitoring.
- Automatic pump control.
- Tank overflow protection.
- Simulation of different water-level conditions.
- Efficient water management.
